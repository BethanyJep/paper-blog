# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Bethany Jep's personal website brings together teaching, building, speaking,
technical resources, personal writing, and exploration. It should reflect the
person behind the work, rather than read only as a professional portfolio.

## Users

Readers discovering Bethany and her work, developers looking for experiments and
learning material, and people exploring her talks and personal reflections.
The relative priority of these audiences remains open.

## Brand Commitments

Bethany confirmed the personality as **playful, warm, curious, and bold**.
Speaking, teaching, and audience moments are the personal material she wants
made more visible. Preserve her name, factual work history, and real content.

Her explicit visual direction is an oldies, analogue feeling inspired by her
LiPlay camera, with animation and graphics. She loves nature, playing, adventure,
whimsy, art, running, teaching, connecting with people, and exploring things.
These personal commitments supersede the earlier proposed visual directions.

Bethany approved the purple preview: plum accents, lilac paper layers, and a
deep-plum teaching panel, retaining butter paper, forest green, and sunflower.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Hugo with the PaperMod theme, Markdown posts and event/project pages, YAML-backed
learning resources and a photo collection. Preserve existing URLs, search, RSS,
theme switching, article readability, and mobile access.

## Evidence on Hand

- `content/events/`: real speaking engagements and workshop accounts.
- `content/projects/`: experiments and their existing demo/repository links.
- `content/posts/`: technical writing and personal reflections.
- `data/content_hub.yaml`: teaching resources, courses, workshops, and recordings.
- `static/assets/images/`: existing speaking and event photography.
- `data/exploring.yaml` and `assets/images/exploring/`: personal exploration.

Do not invent testimonials, audience quotes, attendance counts, achievements,
new capabilities, or event details.

## Product Principles

- Introduce Bethany the person alongside her professional work.
- Let real teaching and community moments demonstrate the personality.
- Invite readers to learn and explore without making navigation a puzzle.
- Keep long-form writing quiet and comfortable to read.
