## Demos

### LLMs as a Service
Maximize the potential of LLMs by providing context and automating documentation tasks.

* [Prompty documentation](https://prompty.ai/)
* [Prompty Schema Documentation](https://nitya.github.io/prompty/documentation/3-Specification/revised/)
* [Prompty tests and examples](https://nitya.github.io/prompty/examples/)

### Agents as a Service
Adding agents to your documentation workflow can automate repetitive tasks, enhance user experience, and provide personalized assistance.

* [Issue labeller agent](https://github.com/BethanyJep/build-your-first-agent)
* [Blog on how to build the issue labeller](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/azuredevcommunityblog/is-it-a-bug-or-a-feature-using-prompty-to-automatically-track-and-tag-issues-/4409327)
* [Implementation on GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/azure-ai-foundry/discussions)

### Scaleability as a Service
Localize your documentation and make it accessible to a wider audience by leveraging AI to translate and adapt content.

* [Co-op Translator](https://github.com/Azure/co-op-translator)
* [Translation on documentation](https://github.com/microsoft/ML-For-Beginners)
* [Blog on translation with co-op translator](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/azuredevcommunityblog/translating-ai-and-ml-for-beginners-curriculums-in-less-than-a-day/4381854)
