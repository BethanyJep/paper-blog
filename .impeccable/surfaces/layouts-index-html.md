# Homepage — Bethany’s analogue field journal

## Status and authority

Finished implementation, documented from `layouts/index.html`. The user explicitly
approved and pinned the LiPlay-inspired oldies/analogue direction and the
playful, warm, curious, bold personality. Her interests include nature, play,
adventure, whimsy, art, running, teaching, connecting and exploring. No further
interview or concept round is required. No original catalogue composition
applies; the user's direction supersedes earlier proposed concepts.
`PRODUCT.md` owns facts; `DESIGN.md` owns the implemented visual system.

## Visitor intent

**Homepage mode: Experience.** Meet the person through real photographic moments,
then follow a curiosity into her work, learning and life.
**Article mode: Read.** Destination articles retain quiet typography and reading
width; this is not a global mandate to make every surface an experience.

## Opening five-section contract

1. **Welcome:** oversized personal greeting left, real interactive photo stack
   right; “Wander a little” leads to `#curiosities`, “Learn with me” to `/content/`.
   Personal links lead to `/about/` and the configured LinkedIn profile.
   The interests ribbon is a transition, not a separate numbered section.
2. **Curiosities:** three coloured notes for building (`/projects/`), personal
   writing (`/archives/`) and exploration (`/exploring/`).
3. **Better together:** deep-plum panel with the real Bengaluru workshop print,
   teaching copy, `/events/`, workshop and watch links.
4. **Journal:** three latest posts sorted by descending date, shown as quiet
   ruled rows with dates, titles and summaries; archive link remains visible.
5. **Outside:** nature/running/adventure copy and the real Susumwa Dam landscape
   print, linking to `/exploring/`.

The newsletter invitation follows these five sections and links to `/newsletter/`.
Do not turn this homepage ordering into a site-wide component rule.

## Interaction and responsive contract

The camera control manually cycles three actual moments with caption links,
polite count announcements and an optional developing effect; no auto-advance.
The first image remains visible without JavaScript. Respect reduced motion and
the explicit pause control. At 700px the paired scenes and curiosity notes stack;
visible wrapped navigation, accessible controls and all destination routes remain.

## Preservation boundary

Keep every existing route, content item, demo/repository link, search, RSS, dark
theme and article reading affordance. Photography and factual captions are
evidence, not placeholders. No new claims, testimonials or invented events.
The delivered world uses local Caprasimo, Gelasio editorial italics, Trebuchet
body, botanical doodles and the actual light/dark tokens in `DESIGN.md`.
