I'm Bethany Jepchumba. I work in Developer Relations at Microsoft (& GitHub), where
building things and sharing what I learn are a big part of my world.

You can [learn with me](/content/), [explore my experiments](/projects/), or
[see where teaching has taken me](/events/).

## Community and leadership

My cup is filled through giving. I am currently a board member at Together For
Africa Organization, where I curate and lead technology engagements, including
software development at Alliance Girls, Mji wa Huruma, and beyond. I also sit on
the board of AIU TVET, championing education.

I dedicate my free time to mentoring and opening up my calendar to KamiLimu and
Global Mentorship Initiative.

## Beyond the browser

Nature, running, art, play, and a little adventure: these belong here just as
much as the technical work. I love exploring things, being outdoors, and leaving
room for whimsy.

My [photo collection](/exploring/) holds some of those moments. My
[writing](/archives/) makes room for reflections on life and everything in
between.
